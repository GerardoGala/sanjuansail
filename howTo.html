Game Guide

Welcome to RollaWord! Your objective is to form intersecting words on the matrix using your mixed tray of letters before the countdown timer hits zero.

  • Select FREE PLAY to roll a randomized hand, or choose DAILY CHALLENGE to play a globally synchronized daily board.
  • Drag and drop your character tiles directly from the bottom row tray up onto the 6x8 grid board cells.
  • Rearrange your letters at any point during active countdown play.
  • When you have linked all your paths successfully together, hit the Done button to freeze the layout and submit your entries for engine calculation.
  • 🧒 RollaKid Mode: In the kids version, there is no countdown timer on the screen or game clock constraints! Children can play completely at their own pace to discover words under a pressure-free environment.

Points are computed based on standard Scrabble metrics, along with strict layout validation rules:

  • Each single letter has an integrated point value shown on the lower-right corner of the tile face. Blank tiles (*) carry zero base points but automatically optimize into any target letter that yields the highest cumulative scoring hand.
  • Color Multipliers: Form a complete valid word using tiles entirely of one matching color to trigger a bonus score overlay! Blue lines apply a x2 multiplier, while Red lines apply a x3 multiplier. These multipliers are clearly added to the individual word calculations.
  • The Moat Rule: All tiles placed onto the playing matrix must form a single, interconnected island block. Separate standalone letter groups are invalid. (Note: This rule does not apply to children in RollaKid mode).
  • Invalid Contamination Cascading: Ensure every single word on the board is a real dictionary term! If an invalid word is discovered, a black line is drawn across it and it scores 0 points. Furthermore, any valid word that intersects or shares a letter tile with that invalid word becomes contaminated and is instantly disqualified.
  • Subtotal Output: The engine instantly sums up the points scored across all valid, non-contaminated words to display a raw SUBTOTAL value before computing final jackpot bonuses.
  • 8-Dice Jackpot Bonus: Get all 8 tray dice onto the board inside completely valid, non-contaminated structures to trigger the ultimate scoring bonus:
    • Adult Mode: If the remaining seconds left to play is greater than 1, your subtotal score gets multiplied by the exact number of remaining seconds left on the clock!
    • RollaKid Mode: Bypasses countdown stress completely to award a happy, flat x2 multiplier bonus directly to their grand total.
